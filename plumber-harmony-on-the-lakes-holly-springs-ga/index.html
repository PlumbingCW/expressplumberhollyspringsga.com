Harmony on the Lakes is one of the better-known residential communities in Holly Springs. The City's own zoning ordinance lists a Harmony on the Lakes Neighborhood Preservation District, and listing sites describe a mix of single-family homes and townhomes around lakes, pools, tennis courts, playgrounds and trails.

That mix matters for plumbing. A detached home and a townhome fail in different ways, and who you call first can depend on whether the water is in your unit or coming through a shared wall. Dispatch is open 24/7, and the price is explained before any work starts.

Detached home or townhome: what changes

The same leak can mean two different phone calls.

Townhome or attached unit

  • Water through a shared wall may start in a neighbor's unit
  • The association may control exterior walls and common areas
  • Tell the neighbor and the association manager if water crosses the wall

Detached single-family home

  • Every pipe and fixture inside the walls is yours to repair
  • The main shutoff protects the whole house
  • Check your association rules before changing anything visible from the street

Common plumbing calls in a newer lake community

Listing sites show most homes here as recent construction, so the usual calls are about wear and use, not old pipe. Here are the three we expect to hear about most.

A tank water heater that has started to drip or run out fast

Look for water at the base, a rumbling sound or a heater that no longer holds heat through a long shower. Our water heater repair page explains when a part is the problem and when the tank is.

Slow sinks and tubs

Hair, soap film and food waste build up in the first few feet of drain. A cable machine usually clears it, and drain cleaning covers what to avoid pouring down in the meantime.

Damp spots on a wall or ceiling

A stain that grows after nobody has used water upstairs deserves a look. Leak detection finds the source without opening more drywall than needed.

Who to call about a leak near the street

Water pooling by the curb or in the meter box is usually on the utility's side. Water inside the house or after your own shutoff valve is yours. Check your bill to see which provider serves your address.

When you are not sure, call and describe where the water is. Dispatch will say honestly whether it sounds like a plumber's job or a utility call.

Before a plumber arrives

  • Find the shutoff for your unit or house and turn it clockwise or a quarter turn
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water
  • Write down where the water is and when it started

Service area and permits

Addresses we checked in Harmony on the Lakes fall inside the City of Holly Springs. A Holly Springs mailing address alone does not prove an address is inside city limits, so for any permit question call the City at 770-345-5536 and give your street.

Larger jobs such as a new water heater or a fixture installation may need a permit, and rules vary by address. Dispatch can point you to the right office. Our service area page lists the other towns and communities we cover.

Harmony on the Lakes plumbing questions

Do you serve Harmony on the Lakes?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7 and covers the neighborhood, including its single-family homes and townhomes. Give your street address and the nearest cross street when you call.

Is Harmony on the Lakes inside Holly Springs city limits?

The City's zoning ordinance names a Harmony on the Lakes district, and the addresses we checked are inside city limits. Some nearby communities share the Holly Springs mailing name without being in the city, so confirm with the City at 770-345-5536 if a permit depends on it.

Who fixes a leak in a townhome shared wall?

Start by shutting off water to your unit and telling the association manager. A plumber can find where the water is coming from, and your insurer or the association will usually decide who pays.

Do you handle water heater problems in Harmony on the Lakes?

Yes. Tank repairs and replacements are common calls. See water heater replacement if the tank itself has failed.

Do I get a price before work starts?

Yes. The plumber explains the cause and gives a clear price before starting, and tells you before doing anything extra.
