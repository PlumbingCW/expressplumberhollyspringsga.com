Terms of Service

The ground rules for using this website and requesting plumbing service by phone.

By using this website or calling our dispatch line you agree to these terms. They are short on purpose.

Service requests

Service is requested by phone. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. Dispatch schedules a licensed plumber based on availability, and arrival times are estimates only.

The price for any job is explained to you before work starts. Work begins only after you agree to the price and scope. If the plumber finds additional problems, you will be told before further work is done.

Website content

Information on this website is general plumbing guidance and is not a substitute for an on-site assessment. Local facts are drawn from public Cherokee County, utility and Georgia sources and may change, so confirm with the agency named on the page.

In a gas leak or life-threatening emergency, leave the building and call 911 or your gas utility first.

Limits and contact

To the extent allowed by law, our liability for any claim is limited to the amount you paid for the service in question. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.

Questions? Call (855) 575-2890 or email contact@expressplumberhollyspringsga.com.
