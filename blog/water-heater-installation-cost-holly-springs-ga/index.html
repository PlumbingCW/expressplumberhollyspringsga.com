Water Heater Installation Cost in Holly Springs, GA

Two quotes for the same tank can differ because of what surrounds the heater. Learn which line items matter and which ones signal a rushed estimate.

  • By the Holly Springs Express Plumbing team
  • Published October 17, 2026
  • 5 min read

The price of the heater itself is usually the smaller part of the decision. What you pay depends on tank size, fuel type, how the heater vents, what code updates the old setup needs, permit handling and the cost of hauling the old unit away. We do not publish a dollar figure, because no honest number exists until someone sees your utility space.

What you can do before any visit is learn the line items and check that each quote covers the same ones. A cheaper quote that skips the expansion tank, drain pan or permit is not cheaper, it only moves the cost to later.

Why two quotes for the same heater are not equal

A quote is a bundle of decisions. One plumber may reuse your old flue, gas shutoff and water connectors, while another replaces them for safety. Both may write the same tank model on the paper and still be doing different amounts of work.

Capacity and fuel come first. A larger tank or a higher-input burner costs more to buy and may need larger piping, and switching from gas to electric, or the reverse, adds work that a like-for-like swap does not. Our water heater installation page describes how we scope each job.

Permits and codes for Holly Springs installs

In unincorporated Cherokee County, plumbing permits come from the Cherokee County Development Service Center, apply through the county's CityView portal and are issued only to state-licensed technicians, at 770-721-7810. If you live in a community like Hickory Flat, that office is the one to ask. Inside the Holly Springs city limits, ask the City at 770-345-5536.

Georgia's state minimum plumbing code is the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with Georgia amendments. In practice that is why the installer should name the permit on the quote, and why a written quote that skips it deserves a question.

Standard versus upgraded installs

A standard install replaces the heater in place. An upgraded one fixes the surroundings too.

Code upgrades that can surprise you

Older installations often fall short of what is required today. Ask the installer which of these apply.

  • A temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe routed to a safe spot.
  • A drain pan under heaters in places where a leak would damage finished floors or living space.
  • A thermal expansion tank where a pressure-reducing valve or backflow device keeps the system closed.
  • A gas shutoff and a sediment trap on gas lines, as the manufacturer's instructions require.
  • Proper vent connectors, clearances and combustion air for gas heaters.
  • Sturdy support and clearances around the heater, as the manufacturer's instructions require.

How to read a quote in four passes

  1. 1

    Match the heater

    Check the brand, model, capacity, fuel and recovery rating. If the quote says only 'standard tank,' ask for the details.

  2. 2

    Check the accessories

    Look for connectors, relief valve, discharge pipe, pan and expansion tank by name. Missing items are future charges.

  3. 3

    Look for the permit and haul-away

    Both should be written down. If the quote is silent, ask who pulls the permit and who removes the old unit.

  4. 4

    Confirm the final price

    Ask what could change the number once the old heater is out. We give a clear price before work starts, and surprises should be agreed first.

Quote that protects you versus one that does not

A thin one-line quote

  • Lists only the heater and labor
  • Says nothing on permit, venting or haul-away
  • Leaves expansion tank and pan open for later
  • Makes comparison between bids impossible

A complete written quote

  • Names the heater and every accessory
  • States who handles the permit and inspection
  • Explains what could change the price
  • Lets you compare line by line

Insurance, timing and honest limits

Homeowner insurance covers sudden water damage in many policies but not the heater that wore out. Ask your agent before you decide, especially if a leak damaged floors or walls. Our water heater replacement page covers what to do when the old tank has already failed.

Same-day appointments are available when the schedule allows, and dispatch is open 24/7 if a heater fails at night. We do not rush a quote, because a rushed installation is the one that causes the next call.

Water heater installation questions

What is included in a standard water heater installation?

Typically the new heater, connections to water and gas or power, a relief valve and discharge pipe, a check of venting, and removal of the old unit. Pan, expansion tank and permit handling vary, so ask for each in writing.

Why does the quote mention an expansion tank?

Heated water expands. If your system has a pressure-reducing valve or backflow device, that water has nowhere to go, and an expansion tank absorbs it. The installer should check your system before deciding.

Should I get more than one quote?

Yes, but compare the same line items. A lower total that leaves out the permit, pan or haul-away is not comparable to a complete one.

Can the installer change my fuel type?

Yes, but it is a larger job. Gas to electric needs a circuit and panel capacity, and electric to gas needs a gas line and vent. Expect the quote to list that extra work.

Who issues the plumbing permit for a water heater in Holly Springs?

Inside the city limits, ask the City of Holly Springs at 770-345-5536. For unincorporated addresses, the Cherokee County Development Service Center at 770-721-7810 issues permits to state-licensed technicians through its CityView portal.
