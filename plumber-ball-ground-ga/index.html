A depot town with marble mills

The Marietta and North Georgia Railroad reached Ball Ground in 1882, built a depot and laid out the town, with lots sold that April. A community that has been repaired for well over a century has mixed pipe materials behind its walls. When a plumber opens a wall in an older house, expect a conversation about what is found there before the plan changes.

Marble finishing became the main industry, with the first marble company opening in 1898 and four finishing mills running in the 1920s. That history is why the historic core still has so many older buildings. For those buildings we start with leak detection using listening and moisture tools, so nobody cuts into a wall on a guess.

Before we arrive in Ball Ground

If a pipe is spraying or a leak is spreading, work through these steps. Each takes under a minute.

  1. Close the main shutoff

    Find the valve where the line enters the house and turn it off, but do not touch the city meter. In older Ball Ground homes the valve may be stiff, so tell dispatch if it will not turn.

  2. Cut power near the water

    Flip off breakers for flooded rooms and the water heater. If the panel is wet or you would have to wade to reach it, stay clear and say so on the call.

  3. Make a clear path

    Clear furniture and rugs from the wet room and the route from the door. Mention steps, tight stairs or a historic entry so the crew can plan how to bring in tools.

  4. Phone the emergency line

    Give the road you are on, such as State Route 5 or Gilmer Ferry Road, and what is leaking. Say if your building is in the historic district.

Water line failures and leaks, our most likely calls here

In a small system fed by one well, a water line break shows up fast, as lost pressure or a muddy flow that clears slowly. If the break is between the meter and your house, the repair is ours, and our water line repair page explains the options. A leak that wets a foundation or crawlspace may need burst pipe repair right away.

Customers in ZIP 30107 can call day or night for either of these, and the same emergency line handles a leak that has gone quiet but left a high bill.

Drains in older houses

Old clay or cast iron drains, common wherever houses date back generations, collect roots and scale. If a drain backs up in a house that old, the drain cleaning page explains cabling and why a camera look is sometimes the better first step.

After-hours sewage backups are emergencies. Keep children and pets out of the room and stop using water.
