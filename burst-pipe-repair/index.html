The first ten minutes after a pipe bursts

Work down the list in order. Each step takes under a minute once you know where things are.

  • Find the main shutoff valve and close it. It is usually where the water line enters the house, at a basement or crawlspace wall, in a garage or by the water heater. A round handle turns clockwise to close, and a lever closes when it sits crosswise to the pipe.
  • If you cannot find it or it will not turn, go to the meter box at the street and close the valve there with a meter key or a pair of pliers. That valve is on the utility side, so use it only in a true emergency.
  • Open the lowest tap in the house and the highest one to drain the pipes, then flush a toilet once.
  • Switch off the water heater at the unit. A gas heater has a control dial, and an electric one has a breaker. A heater with no incoming water can overheat.
  • Turn off power to any room with standing water around outlets, but only if you can reach the breaker without stepping in water. Otherwise stay out and call.
  • Take photos and video of the burst pipe, the wet areas and your damaged belongings before you move anything. Your insurer will ask for them.

Why pipes crack, split and burst

Freezing is the cause people expect, and it does happen. Water expands as it turns to ice and splits a pipe wall, often in an uninsulated crawlspace, an exterior wall or an attic. The break usually shows up when the ice thaws and the water flows again.

Many bursts have nothing to do with cold. Old galvanized pipe corrodes from the inside and thins until it gives way. Copper can pinhole from the water side, a high water pressure setting can stress fittings over years, and a bad solder joint or a pipe rubbing on a stud can fail without warning.

A sudden pressure surge, sometimes called water hammer, can also split a weak joint. If your pipes bang when a valve shuts quickly, tell the plumber. Repairing one break without checking why it happened invites the next one.

How a burst pipe repair goes

The order matters, because repairing a pipe that is still wet or under pressure rarely works.

  1. 1

    Confirm the water is off and the area is safe

    We check the main valve, drain the line and look for water near electrical outlets before touching a tool.

  2. 2

    Find the full extent of the damage

    A burst pipe often has weak pipe on either side of the break. We inspect nearby runs and fittings, and we use a moisture meter if the water went into a wall or ceiling.

  3. 3

    Cut, replace and test

    The failed section comes out and new pipe and fittings go in. We restore pressure slowly and check every joint for seepage before closing up.

  4. 4

    Walk through what comes next

    We show you the repair, point out any other weak pipe and explain which cleanup and drywall work is a separate job.

Drain pipe cracks and leaking drain lines

A cracked drain pipe behaves differently from a supply pipe. It does not spray, it drips or seeps wherever waste water passes, and the smell or a stain on a ceiling below a bathroom may be the first clue. Cast iron and old clay or fiber pipe are the usual suspects in older homes.

We find the crack with a drain camera if the line is accessible. For a section inside a wall or under a floor, a repair means opening that area and replacing the pipe. Longer runs with several cracks are covered under sewer line repair.

Temporary patch versus a proper pipe repair

Clamps and slip-on sleeves can slow a leak for a few hours or days. They are a stopgap, not a plan.

A clamp or wrap from the hardware store

  • Holds only while the pipe is still sound around it
  • Hides how far the damage extends
  • Often fails again under pressure or heat
  • Leaves wet wood and drywall to rot behind it

A cut-out and proper replacement

  • Removes the damaged pipe entirely
  • Joints are tested under full pressure
  • Weak nearby pipe is found and addressed
  • You hear the price before the repair starts

What affects the price of burst pipe repair

We give a clear price before work starts. Many homeowner policies cover water damage from a sudden burst, though gradual leaks, neglect and some freeze-related losses can be treated differently. Call your insurer early, keep your photos and keep any damaged parts and receipts.

Pipe material and size

A short piece of half-inch PEX is quick. A stretch of cast iron or large-diameter copper takes more time and material.

Where the break is

A pipe in an open garage wall is easier than one behind a tiled shower or above a finished ceiling.

How much pipe is weak

One clean split is a small job. Old pipe that is failing along its length may call for a longer replacement.

Access work

Cutting drywall and patching it back are separate steps with their own labor.

After the water is off, what to do about the damage

Start drying within a day or two. Move wet rugs out, open windows if the weather allows, run fans and a dehumidifier, and remove soaked drywall or insulation that stays wet. Mold can start in warm, damp rooms within a couple of days.

Plumbers fix pipes, and water mitigation companies dry and rebuild. We repair the plumbing and tell you what we saw, and that helps your adjuster and your drying contractor. If the pipe burst in an unseen spot, our leak detection page explains how we locate the source.

Pipes and permits in Holly Springs

If your water bill comes from the Cherokee County Water and Sewerage Authority (CCWSA), the authority owns the service line from its main to the meter, and you own everything from the meter to the building. A broken pipe inside the house is your repair. A break in the yard on the building side of the meter belongs to you too, while one on the meter side belongs to the utility, so call CCWSA at 770-479-1813 if you are unsure.

Georgia's minimum plumbing code is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with state amendments. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Holly Springs at 770-345-5536 if your address is in the city. In unincorporated areas such as Hickory Flat, the Cherokee County Development Service Center at 770-721-7810 handles permits and allows emergency work to proceed with the permit obtained as soon as possible.

Never go into standing water near electricity

Water in a room with live outlets or an appliance can carry current. Stay out, shut off power at the panel only from dry ground and call 911 if you see sparks, smell burning or cannot reach the panel safely. If you smell gas at any point, leave the building first and call the gas utility or 911 before calling a plumber.

Burst pipe questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Holly Springs?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7, including holidays. Tell the dispatcher you have a burst pipe, whether the main is off and where the water is going. They will arrange the earliest appointment available and you hear the price before work starts.

How do I shut off the water if I cannot find the main valve?

Look near the water heater, in the garage, at the crawlspace wall and where the line enters the house. If you cannot find it, use the meter box valve at the street in a true emergency. Tell dispatch you could not locate the house valve so the plumber brings the right tools.

Can a cracked pipe be repaired or does it need replacing?

A single clean crack in sound pipe can be cut out and repaired. If the pipe is thin, corroded or cracked in several places, replacing a longer section is the better choice.

What do I do about a cracked drain pipe?

Stop using the fixtures that feed it and call. A camera inspection finds the crack, and a cracked section is cut out and replaced. Water leaking from a drain carries waste, so keep the area clean and dry.

Is a copper pipe leak worth repairing or should I replace it?

One failed joint or a freeze split is usually worth repairing. Several pinholes along the run suggest the copper is worn, and a longer replacement or a repipe may cost less over time.

Will my insurance cover a burst pipe?

Many policies cover sudden water damage and some also cover access, but they differ on gradual leaks and on the pipe itself. Take photos before cleanup, keep receipts and call your agent early.
