Emergency garbage disposal repair in Holly Springs starts with our 24/7 dispatch line, and a licensed plumber is sent as soon as one is free. You hear the price before work starts.

Before you call, switch the disposal off at the wall and keep your hands out of the sink opening. The safe checks below cover the most common jams.

Disposal jammed or flooding the cabinet? Safe first steps

Power off, hands out, then look for the simple cause. These steps fix a surprising number of jams.

  1. 1

    Turn off the switch and unplug it

    Flip the wall switch off. Then reach under the sink and unplug the cord, or switch off the circuit breaker if the unit is wired in. A disposal that starts while you work can cause serious injury.

  2. 2

    Turn the blades by hand from underneath

    Look at the bottom of the disposal for a small hex-shaped socket in the center. Fit the hex key that came with the unit, or a one-quarter-inch Allen key, and work it back and forth until it turns a full circle.

  3. 3

    Remove what jammed it from above

    With the power off, use tongs or pliers and a flashlight to lift out the spoon, bone or bottle cap. Never reach into the opening with your hand.

  4. 4

    Press the red reset button

    There is a small red button on the bottom of the unit. It pops out when the motor overheats. Wait a few minutes, press it in, restore power and test with a little cold water.

Humming but not spinning

A steady hum with no movement means the motor has power but the blades are stuck. The hex key step above frees most of these. If the blades turn easily by hand and the unit still only hums, the motor or its starting capacitor may have failed, and that is a replacement.

If there is no hum and no movement at all, check the reset button, the breaker and the outlet. A disposal that trips its breaker again as soon as you turn it on has a shorted motor and is done.

Where the water is coming from

Run a little water and watch. Dry the unit first with a towel so you can see the first drop.

From the sink flange at the top

Water at the ring where the sink meets the disposal means the flange seal or mounting has loosened. The fix is to reset the mount with new plumber's putty or a gasket.

From the side or bottom of the body

A leak from the unit's housing or a hairline crack in the casing cannot be repaired. This is a replacement, because rust and wear have gone through the shell.

From the drain pipe connection

A weeping joint on the elbow that leads to the sink trap can often be fixed by tightening the connection or replacing the gasket. It is a modest repair.

From the dishwasher hose

A loose clamp on the small hose that feeds in from the dishwasher can drip under the sink and look like the disposal itself.

A clog in the disposal or in the sink drain

A disposal that grinds fine but leaves the sink full of water has a drain problem past the unit. Grease and food fibers build up in the trap and the pipe behind it. Running the disposal harder only pushes more material into the same restriction.

If both sides of a double sink are slow, or the dishwasher backs up into the sink, the clog sits in the shared branch line. Our drain cleaning page covers how those lines are cabled. We check the disposal and the drain at the same visit so you are not called back for the second problem.

What can go down, and what cannot

A disposal is a grinder for soft scraps, not a trash can. These rules protect both the motor and your drain.

Repair or replace the disposal

A disposal is a moving machine with a limited life. Age and the type of failure usually decide.

Repair is a fit when

  • The jam clears and the motor spins freely
  • The leak is at the flange or a drain connection
  • The unit is fairly new and quiet
  • A dishwasher hose or clamp is the real problem

Replace when

  • The housing leaks or the casing is rusted through
  • The motor hums with the blades free, or trips the breaker
  • The unit jams repeatedly or grinds poorly
  • It is more than about ten years old and failing

Installing a new disposal

A new disposal mounts to the sink flange with a snap ring, connects to the drain elbow and to power, and often connects the dishwasher hose. If the sink has no power at the cabinet, an electrician may need to add an outlet or a switch, and we will say so before starting.

A new dishwasher connection has one detail that trips up many installs. Most disposals have a knockout plug inside the dishwasher inlet, and it has to be punched out with a screwdriver and hammer before the dishwasher drains. If the plug is left in, the dishwasher will not empty. We take the plug out and fish out the piece.

Never put your hand in a disposal

A unit can start if someone flips the switch while your hand is inside, and the grinding parts can crush fingers. Use tongs, pliers or the hex key from beneath, and keep power off at the plug or the breaker the whole time. If you cannot see what is stuck, stop and call.

Garbage disposal questions

Do you handle garbage disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Holly Springs?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7 for leaking, jammed or dead disposals. Tell the dispatcher whether water is reaching the cabinet floor, and they will arrange the earliest appointment available. You hear the price before work starts.

Why is my disposal humming but not spinning?

The blades are probably stuck on something. Turn off power, then use the hex socket on the bottom of the unit to rotate them. If they free up and the unit still hums, the motor may be worn out.

Is a leaking disposal fixable?

It depends where it leaks. A loose flange or a worn drain gasket can be fixed. A crack or a rusted-through housing cannot, and the unit needs replacing.

Can I use chemical drain cleaner when the sink is clogged?

Please do not. Chemicals sit in the sink and splash when we open the trap. Tell the plumber if you already used one. A cable machine clears the line without chemicals. See drain cleaning.

Should I run cold or hot water through the disposal?

Cold water. It keeps grease solid, so it can be ground and carried away. Hot water melts fat that then re-hardens in the pipe.

Can I put a disposal on a septic system?

Ask first. A tank that receives food solids needs pumping more often. Call the North Georgia Health District at 770-479-0444 for guidance on your system.
