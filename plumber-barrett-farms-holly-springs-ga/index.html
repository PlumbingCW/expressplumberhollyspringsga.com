Planning a replacement before it becomes an emergency

A tank heater that leaks can empty onto a garage or hall floor. Checking the heater label for its age, and watching for rust or moisture at the base, lets you schedule a visit instead of waiting for a failure.

Same-day appointments are offered when the schedule allows. A planned swap also lets you compare tank and tankless options before deciding.

Is my water heater too old?

Age alone is not proof. The heater label shows the build date, and a plumber can check the tank, valves and anode. Rust, leaks or slow recovery are stronger signs.

Should I replace all my pipes?

Not by default. A plumber looks first, and whole-house work comes up only if the pipes show a real problem. See repiping for when it applies.
