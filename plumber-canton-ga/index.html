Canton plumbing problems that cannot wait until Monday

Some calls can sit until a normal appointment. These four cannot, and each one is an emergency worth a call at any hour.

Water spreading from a supply line or fixture

A split braided hose under a sink or a failed shutoff valve can put gallons on the floor in an hour. Close the fixture stop or the main valve, then call. Our burst pipe repair page explains the repair that follows.

Several drains backing up together

When the tub gurgles as the toilet flushes, the main drain is probably blocked. Stop running water and call the emergency line, because every extra flush sends more wastewater toward the lowest drain in the house.

A water heater leaking from the tank

A drip at a fitting is one problem. Water seeping from the tank body or base means the tank itself has failed. Close the cold supply valve, switch off power or gas control if you can do it safely, and call. If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 before anyone else.

Sewage smell or dark water at a floor drain

Dark, foul water rising from a floor drain is wastewater, not rinse water. Keep children and pets out of the room and avoid skin contact until a plumber has cleared the line.

Before we arrive at your Canton address

If you have a burst pipe or an active leak, these four steps limit the damage while the 24/7 line gets a plumber moving.

  1. Shut off the main water valve

    It is usually where the line enters the house or in a box near the street. Turn a gate valve clockwise until it stops, or turn a ball valve a quarter turn. Leave the utility's meter alone and use your own valve.

  2. Kill power to wet areas

    If water has reached outlets, a garage water heater or the panel, flip the breaker only if you can reach it without standing in water. If you cannot, stay out and tell dispatch.

  3. Clear the immediate area

    Lift rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water, and open a path from the front door to the leak. Leave a parking space near the entrance for the plumber's tools.

  4. Call the 24/7 line

    Give your street, what is leaking and whether you closed a valve. If your address is hard to find, name the nearest road, such as GA 20, GA 140 or an I-575 exit.

Drain backups and what we do about them

Most backups start the same way. One fixture slows, a second gurgles, and then the lowest drain in the house fills. A cable machine opens the line, and when the clog keeps returning, a sewer camera inspection shows whether roots, a sag or a cracked joint is behind it.

Grease and wipes sold as flushable are common causes inside a house. If neighbors report the same backup, the street main may be involved. Our drain cleaning page covers the in-house side.

Calls from ZIP 30114 and the PO box ZIP 30169 reach the same dispatch line, and both are served for drain cleaning and water heater repair at any hour.

Water heaters that fail without warning

Tank heaters fail by rusting through, and the failure usually arrives as a puddle, not a slow fade. Look for water at the base, rust-colored hot water or a rumbling noise. A heater that is leaking from the tank cannot be patched, so the choice is between repairing the parts that failed and replacing the unit.

On any gas model, follow the manufacturer's instructions, and if you smell gas, leave the building first. A dead heater with no leak can usually wait for the next appointment, and dispatch will say so honestly.
