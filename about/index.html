About Holly Springs Express Plumbing

A 24/7 plumbing dispatch line for Holly Springs, Georgia, built around clear answers and clear prices.

24/7 emergency line(855) 575-2890 Call now

Holly Springs Express Plumbing exists for the moment something goes wrong: water on the floor, a drain that will not clear, a water heater that has quit. You call one number, any hour, and dispatch sends a licensed and insured plumber to your address.

We serve Holly Springs, GA and the nearby Cherokee County towns of Canton, Hickory Flat and Ball Ground. Our address is 2800 Holly Springs Pkwy, Holly Springs, GA 30115.

How we work

Every call starts with questions about what you see, hear and smell, followed by plain instructions to keep your home safe while help is on the way. The plumber explains the cause, the options and the price before work starts, so you decide with the facts in hand.

We say what we do not know. If a leak needs a camera or a listening device to locate, we say so rather than guess. Our leak detection and sewer inspection pages describe those methods.

Licensing and how to check it

Georgia licenses master and journeyman plumbers through the Secretary of State's Construction Industry Licensing Board, and you can look up any plumber's license online. We encourage you to check anyone you hire, including us. Our guide to hiring a licensed plumber walks through what to look for.

We do not publish individual technician names or license numbers on this site, because the plumber assigned to your call depends on the time and place. Ask dispatch for the assigned plumber's license details when you book and they will provide them.

What we do not do

We do not give prices without hearing the problem, and we do not promise arrival times. We do not handle gas leaks by phone. If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 first.

Septic system questions belong to the North Georgia Health District at 770-479-0444. If your home is on septic, tell us when you call so the right plumber is sent.

What a visit looks like from start to finish

Whether it is a midnight burst pipe or a planned water heater swap, the visit follows the same four steps.

  1. 1

    The call

    Dispatch asks what you see, hear and smell, gives you shutoff and safety instructions, and books the earliest visit that fits.

  2. 2

    The diagnosis

    The plumber looks at the fixture, line or heater, tests it and explains the cause in plain words before touching anything.

  3. 3

    The price

    You hear the price and the scope before work starts. You can say yes, ask questions or decide to wait.

  4. 4

    The repair and check

    The work is finished, the system is tested under normal use, and you are told what to watch for in the next few days.

Five checks before you hire any plumber in Georgia

These apply to us and to every other company you consider.

  • Ask for the plumber's name and license details, then look them up through the Secretary of State's online license lookup.
  • Get the price and scope of work stated before work begins, not after.
  • Ask whether a permit is needed. In the city of Holly Springs, the City permit office can tell you, and for unincorporated parts of Cherokee County the Development Service Center at 770-721-7810 does.
  • Be cautious of anyone who asks for full payment up front or cannot say where they are based.
  • Keep a written record of what was done, including parts replaced and any warranty the manufacturer provides.

How this site is built and what it will not claim

This website is call-only on purpose. It has no contact forms, no star ratings and no customer counts, because we would rather show you how a repair works than ask you to trust a number. The local facts on each page come from Cherokee County, the Cherokee County Water and Sewerage Authority and State of Georgia sources, and each blog post names the type of source it used.

When we cannot verify something, we tell you to test your tap or ask your water utility. That is why you will see phrases like if your bill comes from CCWSA, instead of a promise about every address.
