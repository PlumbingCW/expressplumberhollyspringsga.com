When a clogged drain is an emergency

A slow sink can wait for a normal appointment. These signs cannot, because they point to a blockage that is still growing or to a line that is already backing up into the house.

Water backs up into a second fixture

You flush the toilet and the shower gurgles, or you run the washer and the floor drain bubbles. Two fixtures reacting together usually means the shared branch or the main line is blocked.

A drain is fully stopped

Nothing moves after a plunger and a few minutes of patience. A fully stopped drain keeps filling every time anyone uses water upstream of it.

Sewage smell or dark water

Dark, foul water coming up through a tub or floor drain is wastewater, not just soap and rinse water. Treat it as a health issue and keep children and pets out of the room.

Water is spreading across flooring

Standing water soaks into subfloors and cabinets within hours. A fast clear limits the damage and the cleanup.

What to do while you wait for the plumber

These steps cost nothing and make the visit faster.

  • Stop using sinks, tubs, showers, the dishwasher and the washing machine until the drain is clear.
  • Do not run the garbage disposal if the sink is backed up. It only pushes the clog deeper.
  • Shut off the main water valve if water is still flowing onto the floor from a fixture.
  • Wear gloves and boots before touching standing water, and keep children and pets out of the room.
  • Move rugs, cardboard and anything absorbent away from the wet area.
  • Note which fixtures are affected. A plumber can read that pattern quickly.

How we find and clear the clog

Every drain call follows the same basic order, so you know what is happening and why.

  1. 1

    Locate the blockage

    We check which fixtures are affected, then work out whether the clog sits in the fixture trap, a branch line or the main drain.

  2. 2

    Clear it with the right tool

    A hand auger handles a sink trap. A cable machine reaches branch lines. Heavier buildup or roots call for a larger cutter head.

  3. 3

    Confirm the line is moving

    We run water through the line and watch the flow. A drain that clears and refills in minutes was not really cleared.

  4. 4

    Explain what caused it

    If a camera inspection or the cable's feel points to roots, a bellied pipe or a broken section, we tell you and show your options.

Kitchen sink clogs

Kitchen drains clog with grease, food fibers and soap film that cool and harden inside the pipe. The blockage often starts in the trap and tailpiece, then creeps back toward the wall. If both sides of a double sink are slow, the clog is past the sink tee rather than in the basket.

Running hot water after a greasy meal does not solve this, because the grease re-cools further down the line. A cable cleans the pipe walls instead of poking a hole through the middle. If the dishwasher drains into the same line, tell us, since it often shares the blockage.

A sink that backs up only when the garbage disposal runs is a separate problem. Our garbage disposal repair page covers jams and failed units.

Slow or stopped shower and tub drains

Hair and soap scum bind together just below the strainer. Most shower clogs sit within the first few feet of pipe, which is why a small drain tool sometimes works for a week and then stops. When water pools around your ankles after a short shower, the clog has usually grown past the reach of a household tool.

Tub drains add a stopper linkage and an overflow plate that can hide buildup. We pull the strainer or stopper assembly first, then cable the line so the whole branch gets cleaned, not only the visible part.

Snaking, cabling and when it is not enough

Snaking and cabling are the same idea at different sizes. A thin cable spins through a trap and branch line and breaks up soft clogs. A heavier machine with a cutter head handles hardened buildup and light root intrusion in larger pipes.

Cabling has limits. It clears a path through the middle of a pipe but leaves a coating on the walls, so grease and sludge return faster. When a line clogs again and again, hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scrub the pipe wall. Jetting is also the better choice for heavy grease in a commercial kitchen line.

If a line clogs repeatedly in the same place, a camera inspection can show the reason. See sewer camera inspection for how that works.

Main line backups and drain line leaks

When the lowest drain in the house backs up first, the blockage is usually in the main line that carries all wastewater out of the building. This is the most urgent kind of drain call. Our sewer line cleaning page explains how main lines are cleared and what happens when roots are involved.

Sometimes a drain keeps clogging because the pipe itself is leaking or broken. Water seeping out of a cracked drain line carries waste with it and leaves solids behind. A damp spot, a patch of soft ground or a persistent sewer smell near one area of the house is worth a camera look.

Chemical cleaners and plungers versus a plumber

A plunger is a fair first move on a single sink or toilet. Bottled drain cleaners are a different story.

Store-bought chemical cleaners

  • Can sit in a stopped pipe for days
  • Burn skin and eyes if the water splashes back
  • Never mix two products, because some combinations release toxic gas
  • Do not remove hair, roots or grease from the pipe wall

A licensed plumber

  • Clears the clog with cable machines, not chemicals
  • Checks the flow before leaving
  • Can add a camera look when clogs keep returning
  • Gives you the price before work starts

Drains in Holly Springs homes

Holly Springs has been an incorporated town since 1906, and today the service area mixes older homes with newer construction along the I-575 corridor. Older drain lines tend to be cast iron or clay on the street side, while newer homes use plastic pipe. That difference changes which cutter head is safe and whether a camera check is worth doing.

Before any drain work, know whether your home is on public sewer or a septic system. If you are not sure, your property records or the utility that bills you can tell you. Septic questions go to the North Georgia Health District at 770-479-0444, because a septic tank problem is not cleared like a home drain. If your home is on a septic system and a drain backs up, call us and say so, since the first step is different.

Georgia's minimum plumbing code is based on the International Plumbing Code (IPC) with state amendments. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Holly Springs at 770-345-5536 before you replace any drain line.

Sewage backups are a health concern

If dark, foul-smelling water comes up through a floor drain or tub, avoid skin contact and keep children and pets away. Do not run fans in a way that spreads moisture to dry rooms. Call dispatch first, then your insurer if there is water damage.

Drain cleaning questions

Do you handle drain cleaning emergencies at night or on weekends in Holly Springs?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7 for stopped drains and backups. Tell the dispatcher which fixtures are affected and whether water is spreading, and they will arrange the earliest appointment available. You hear the price before any work starts.

Why does my drain keep clogging after I clear it?

A drain that clogs again in days or weeks usually has buildup on the pipe walls, a belly in the line where water sits, or roots entering a joint. A cable opens a path but leaves the coating behind. A camera inspection or hydro jetting is the next step.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaner before you arrive?

We recommend against it. Chemicals can burn skin and eyes when we open the pipe, and mixing products can release dangerous gas. If you already used one, tell the plumber which product and when.

Can you clear a kitchen sink and the dishwasher line together?

Yes. They often share a branch, so we check both. If the dishwasher drain is separate, we confirm that it drains freely before we leave.

Should I worry if more than one drain is slow?

Yes. Several slow fixtures at once point to a blockage downstream of all of them, often the main line. Stop using water and call. A main line clog gets worse each time water goes down the pipes.

Do you clear drains for businesses?

Yes. Restaurants, shops and offices have grease, paper and floor drain problems that need heavier equipment. See our commercial plumbing page for details.
