What to do when a toilet or sink keeps clogging

  1. 1

    Try a plunger on the fixture itself

    A flange plunger works on toilets and a cup plunger on sinks. Do two or three firm passes, not twenty.

  2. 2

    Check other drains

    If the tub or a second toilet also slows, the blockage is probably past the branch line, not in one fixture.

  3. 3

    Skip the chemical cleaners

    They can sit in a standing pipe and harm fittings. Tell the plumber if you already used one.

  4. 4

    Call and describe what drains slowly

    The answer decides whether it is a quick drain cleaning visit or a camera look at the line.
