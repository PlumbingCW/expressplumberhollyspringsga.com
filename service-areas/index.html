Why each town has its own page

Plumbing problems are the same underneath, but the utilities, permit offices and housing mix differ. Canton runs its own water and sewer department, Ball Ground runs a city water system, and Hickory Flat is unincorporated, so a permit there goes through the county. A single combined page would hide those differences.

That is also why we ask if your home is on public sewer or septic when you call. Septic questions go to the North Georgia Health District at 770-479-0444, and the answer changes which plumber and which tools are sent.
